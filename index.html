01

The inbox: what arrives this week

Customer POs, a supplier invoice (and a reminder email for the same invoice), an inventory count and your own open POs. Normally someone retypes all of this by hand. Every document is editable: change a quantity or price and re-run to see the checks react.

02

Clean data, one format

Every line item pulled into consistent tables. Stray spaces, mixed date formats, typos in part numbers, "$1,200"-style text numbers, spelling variants and duplicates are fixed automatically. Highlighted cells were cleaned.

03

Cross-checks and dashboard

Once the data is clean, the system checks invoices against your POs, checks orders against inventory, and flags anything that needs a person.
